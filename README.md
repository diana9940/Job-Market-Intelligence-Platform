# Job-Market-Intelligence-Platform

The project aims to develop a data-driven platform that collects job postings from
multiple sources and transforms them into meaningful insights about the current job
market.

The platform will analyze job roles, required skills, technologies, experience levels,
locations, and other relevant job attributes. It will also explore trends in job and skill
demand and may incorporate predictive models to identify potential future market
trends based on the available historical data.

The collected and processed information will be presented through interactive
dashboards and a user-friendly platform, allowing users to explore job-market
requirements across different career fields.
